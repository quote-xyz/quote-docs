# Hyperliquid Builder Codes

Quote leverages Builder Codes to offer its users access to the underlying market. Builder codes allow builders (like Quote) to receive a fee on fills that they send on behalf of a user.

As part of onboarding to Quote, the user must approve a maximum Quote builder fee, and can revoke permissions at any time. Builder codes are processed entirely on-chain as part of the fee logic.

More information about Quote fees is available in the Fee Schedule, and more information about Builders Codes is available in the [Hyperliquid docs](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/builder-codes).
