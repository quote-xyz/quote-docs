# Trading with Privacy

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-01-18 at 1.01.26 pm.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Figure 6. Quote Privacy Features for Private Orders.</p></figcaption></figure>

Quote's passive strategies have a set of anti-gaming features to minimize information leakage. The schedule and sizes posted to exchanges are randomized, and an algorithmic model is used to filter liquidity used to determine order placement.&#x20;

Resting orders remain confined to the Quote Execution Engine until placed on HyperCore, ensuring that the parent trade is never fully displayed until fully executed.
