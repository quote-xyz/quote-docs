# Fee Schedule

### Quote Alpha

Fees are applied to trading orders, 2 bps (0.02%) for perpetual derivatives and 4 bps (0.04%) for spot. Unlike normal trading desks, when using Quote, there are no fixed subscriptions or minimum monthly payments.

Quote's fee structure applies on top of [Hyperliquid's fee structure](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/fees), as per [Builder Codes design](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/builder-codes).
