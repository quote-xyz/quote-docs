# Contact Us

For anything about the Quote application or questions about the protocol, the team is reachable in the official [Telegram community](https://t.me/quotemarkets).

The terminal also has an in-app support chat, which is the quickest route for an account-specific problem because it carries your context with it.

## Before you write

You will get a faster answer with these to hand:

- Your wallet address.
- The approximate time the problem happened, with a timezone.
- A transaction hash, if funds are involved.
- What you expected against what you saw.

Several common problems are already answered here: [deposit or transfer issues](deposit-or-transfer-issues.md), [connectivity issues](connectivity-issues.md), and [a compromised account](account-compromised.md).

{% hint style="danger" %}
Nobody from Quote will ever ask for your seed phrase or private key, or send you a direct message first asking you to install software or open a link. Anyone doing so is an attacker, whatever their display name says. Verify every channel against [Official Links](official-links.md).
{% endhint %}
