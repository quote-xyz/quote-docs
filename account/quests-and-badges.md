# Quests and Badges

Quests are tasks. Badges are what you get for finishing a themed set of them. Together they record what kind of user you are, which is a different question from how much you trade.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Badges are not [tiers](rewards-tiers.md). A tier is set by your 30-day volume and changes your referral, L2, and cashback rates. A badge is earned by completing quests and changes no rates at all.
{% endhint %}

## Quests

Each quest has a target and your progress toward it, and completes once you reach the target. Completion is sticky: once done, a quest stays done.

The catalog is evaluated server-side every time it is read, so quests covering things you have already done complete on their own without you claiming anything. They cover actions like funding your account, placing your first trade, connecting X or Telegram, referring other users, and reaching volume thresholds with the execution engine. One asks you to submit a favourite quote.

Your profile ranks the incomplete quests you can actually act on and surfaces the best next one, ordered by how close you are to finishing it and to finishing the badge it belongs to.

{% hint style="info" %}
Connecting X completes its quest once the identity token attesting the link is reissued, which happens shortly after the link itself. If the badge does not appear immediately, wait a few seconds rather than reconnecting.
{% endhint %}

## Badges

Every quest belongs to exactly one badge, and a badge is earned when all of its member quests are complete. There are seven: **Prologue**, **Ghostwriter**, **Polyglot**, **Cartographer**, **Editor**, **Typesetter**, and **Chorus**.

Each groups a themed set of quests. Your profile shows the full case, earned and unearned alike, with the member quests of each badge and your progress on them, so open a badge to see what it asks for. An unearned badge is drawn in the same shape as an earned one so you can see what is left to do.

When you earn a badge, the terminal celebrates it once. The acknowledgement is recorded server-side, so the celebration does not replay on another device or after clearing your browser storage.
