# Referrals

The referral program pays you a share of the fees generated by people you bring to Quote, across two layers, at rates set by your [rewards tier](rewards-tiers.md).

## Your code and your seats

You claim one referral code, and you choose its name. The name is required, there is no random fallback, and it is **immutable once set**, so pick it deliberately.

A code admits a limited number of people, counted in **seats**:

- You start with a base grant of seats.
- You earn further seats as your own cumulative Quote-routed volume grows, one per fixed increment of volume.
- Once no seats remain, the code stops admitting anyone until you earn another.

Some accounts hold a fixed operator-set grant instead. If yours does, your seat count is set directly and does not move with your volume, so the trade-more-to-unlock-a-seat progress does not apply to you.

## The two layers

| Layer | Who it covers | What you earn |
|---|---|---|
| **L1**, direct | People who joined with your code | Your tier's referral rate on their fees |
| **L2**, indirect | People *they* referred | Your tier's L2 override on their fees |

L2 covers wallets referred by your referees after the two-layer program launched, so an older downline does not retroactively produce L2 earnings.

Your profile breaks earnings down by layer, and lists both your direct referees and your indirect ones, with the volume and fees each has generated and what you earned from them.

## The invited-user rebate

Being referred pays you too. If you joined through a real referrer, you receive a flat rebate on **your own** fees, separate from your tier's cashback and set program-wide rather than by tier.

It is not unconditional. It stops accruing once your cumulative volume passes the program cap, and it does not apply if you did not come through a genuine referrer. Your profile shows whether it is still active for you.

## Claiming

Earnings accumulate as a claimable balance and pay out in USDC.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
#### Accrue

Referral earnings, L2 overrides, and cashback all accrue into your claimable balance as the fees they derive from are paid.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Claim

Claim your full claimable balance once it clears the program's minimum. There is a minimum claim amount, so a small balance has to build before it can be withdrawn.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Payout

A claim enqueues a USDC payout to your wallet. It moves from pending to sent when it settles.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
A failed payout returns its earnings to your claimable balance, so nothing is lost. Your claim history stays available so you can see what happened.
{% endhint %}

Payouts can be switched off at the program level. When they are, earnings continue to accrue and claiming is unavailable until they are switched back on.

## Over the API

Referral endpoints are gated to terminal sessions and are not callable with an API key, so they are not part of the [Trader API reference](../api-reference/introduction.md). Manage referrals in the terminal.
